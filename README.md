## 👋 A little about me

Hey, I'm an Information Technology graduate currently pursuing my BSc Honours in Computing at UNISA.

I'm fascinated by the space where **technology meets people, business, and the future**. From understanding how systems work to exploring how AI can make them smarter, I'm always looking for connections between ideas that might seem unrelated at first.

My journey so far has taken me through business systems, software engineering, information security, human-computer interaction, enterprise architecture, and drone technology.

And honestly? I think the most interesting things happen when these worlds collide. ⚡

## 🚀 Things I'm building, exploring & imagining

> Not every idea starts as a finished product. Some start with a question, a problem, or a late-night "what if?"

### 🐝 Beelas — Smarter systems. Faster buzz.

An early-stage software and automation venture exploring how connected workflows, reporting, visibility, and maintenance processes can work better together.

The bigger idea? Less friction. Smarter operations. More room to innovate.

**Status:** Early-stage venture and concept development.

### 💸 CoinBliss — Making money management more approachable

A personal finance application concept developed from an interest in human-computer interaction and designing technology around real human needs.

Because useful technology shouldn't just work. It should make sense to the people using it.

**Status:** Concept and design project.

### 🔐 Information Security Policy Compliance Research

My Honours research explores the factors influencing information security policy compliance, bringing together behavioural, technological, and organisational perspectives.

I'm particularly interested in understanding why people comply, why they don't, and how organisations can create more effective security practices.

**Status:** Honours research in progress.
